A **leave application** is a short formal request to your manager or HR for time off. Indian workplaces still expect a written application — usually an email or an HR-portal request — even for a single day of leave, because it creates a record for payroll and leave-balance accounting.

This guide gives you clean, copy-ready **leave application formats for office** for every common situation: sick leave, casual leave, earned leave, half day, marriage, maternity and family emergencies. ## The basic format of a leave application

Whatever the leave type, every application needs five things:

| Element | Example |
|---|---|
| Subject line | "Leave Application: 2 Days Casual Leave (27–28 Oct)" |
| Leave type and dates | Casual leave, 27 and 28 October 2026 (2 days) |
| Brief reason | One line is enough — no long explanations |
| Work-coverage plan | Who covers you, or what you've completed |
| Contact availability | Reachable on phone / fully offline |

Address it to your reporting manager, keep HR in CC if that's your company's practice, and send it **before** the leave whenever possible.

## Sick leave application for office

> **Subject: Sick Leave Application – [Your Name] ([Date])**
>
> Dear [Manager's name],
>
> I am unwell with fever and will not be able to come to office today, [date]. Kindly grant me sick leave for [1/2] day(s). I will keep my phone available for anything urgent, and [colleague's name] has visibility on my pending tasks.
>
> I will share a medical certificate if required.
>
> Regards,
> [Name] · [Employee ID]

Most Indian companies require a medical certificate only for sick leave beyond 2–3 consecutive days — check your HR policy. For a longer absence, state the expected return date and update your manager if it changes.

## Casual leave application (one day / two days)

> **Subject: Casual Leave – 1 Day ([Date])**
>
> Dear [Manager's name],
>
> I need to attend to some personal work and request one day of casual leave on [date]. I have completed my scheduled deliverables for the day, and I will be reachable on phone for anything urgent.
>
> Regards,
> [Name]

Casual leave (CL) in India is typically 7–12 days a year, meant for short personal needs, and usually cannot be clubbed with earned leave. Keep the reason to one line — "personal work" is acceptable.

## Earned / privilege leave application (vacation)

> **Subject: Earned Leave Application – [X] Days ([Start] to [End])**
>
> Dear [Manager's name],
>
> I would like to apply for [X] days of earned leave from [start date] to [end date] for a family vacation. I have planned the handover: [colleague's name] will handle [responsibility] in my absence, and I will complete [milestone] before I leave. Kindly approve.
>
> Regards,
> [Name]

For planned long leave, apply at least 2–4 weeks in advance and confirm the approval in writing before booking travel.

## Half day leave application

> **Subject: Half Day Leave – [Date] (Second Half)**
>
> Dear [Manager's name],
>
> I have a personal appointment on [date] afternoon and request half-day leave for the second half. I will be in office till 1 PM and will finish [task] before leaving.
>
> Regards,
> [Name]

## Other common situations

- **Marriage leave:** state the wedding date and attach the invitation if your company requires it; typical grant is 3–15 days depending on policy.
- **Maternity leave:** under the Maternity Benefit Act, eligible women employees get up to 26 weeks of paid leave for the first two children (12 weeks thereafter). Apply in writing with the expected delivery date and your planned start of leave.
- **Paternity leave:** not mandated by central law for the private sector, but many companies offer 5–15 days — check your policy and apply like casual leave.
- **Emergency / bereavement leave:** inform your manager by phone or message immediately, then send the written application when you can. Mention the emergency briefly; companies are generally flexible with documentation.

## Tips that get your leave approved faster

1. **Apply early** for anything planned — last-minute requests are the top reason for rejection.
2. **Propose coverage.** A one-line handover plan ("Rahul will handle client calls") removes your manager's main objection.
3. **Check your leave balance first** in the HR portal so you request the right leave type.
4. **One leave, one email.** Don't mix leave requests with status updates or other asks.
5. **Confirm approval in writing** before you book tickets or go offline.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I write a leave application for office?

Keep it to 3–4 sentences: state the leave type and exact dates, give a one-line reason, mention who covers your work, and say whether you'll be reachable. Use a clear subject line like "Casual Leave – 1 Day (27 Oct)" and send it to your manager before the leave.

### What is the difference between casual leave, sick leave and earned leave?

Casual leave (CL) is for short personal needs, typically 7–12 days a year. Sick leave (SL) is for illness, usually 10–12 days a year, with a medical certificate needed for longer stretches. Earned leave (EL/PL) accrues as you work, is meant for planned vacations, and is often encashable or carried forward as per state Shops & Establishments rules and company policy.

### Can I send a leave application by WhatsApp?

For emergencies, informing your manager by WhatsApp or a call first is fine, but follow it up with a formal email or HR-portal request so the leave is recorded for payroll. A chat message alone usually doesn't count as an official application.

### Is a medical certificate required for sick leave?

Usually only when sick leave exceeds 2–3 consecutive days, though this varies by company policy. For one day of fever or a cold, a simple application is generally enough.

### How many days of leave am I entitled to in India?

It depends on your state's Shops and Establishments Act and company policy, but a common structure is 12–21 days of earned leave, 7–12 days of casual leave and 10–12 days of sick leave per year, plus national and festival holidays.

### Can my manager reject my leave application?

Yes, leave (other than statutory entitlements like maternity leave) is subject to approval and business needs. Applying early, keeping requests reasonable and proposing work coverage greatly improve approval chances.
