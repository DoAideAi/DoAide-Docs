## Why you need rent receipts at all

If you receive **House Rent Allowance (HRA)** as part of your salary, rent receipts are your proof of actually paying rent, which lets your employer give you the HRA exemption under Section 10(13A) of the Income Tax Act and deduct less TDS from your salary. Without receipts, the full HRA is taxed.

Two notes before you start:

- **The HRA exemption is available under the old tax regime.** If you have opted for the new regime (the default since FY 2023-24), HRA is taxable regardless of receipts — one of the factors in the old vs new decision.
- **Receipts also help beyond tax**: as address proof for passports, bank KYC, and as a payment record if a deposit dispute ever reaches an argument.

7. **Landlord's PAN, if annual rent exceeds ₹1,00,000** (more below)
8. **Revenue stamp, for cash payments above ₹5,000** (more below)

## The revenue stamp rule, explained

A ₹1 revenue stamp is required when the rent is **paid in cash and the amount exceeds ₹5,000**. The landlord signs across the stamp. If you pay by UPI, bank transfer or cheque — as most tenants now do — **no revenue stamp is needed**, because the bank record itself evidences the payment. Generate the receipt, and if you are a cash payer, stick the stamp before the landlord signs.

## The landlord PAN rule (₹1 lakh per year)

If your total annual rent exceeds **₹1,00,000** (about ₹8,334 per month), your employer must collect your **landlord's PAN** along with the receipts. No PAN means the employer cannot allow the exemption on the excess. If your landlord does not have a PAN, they must give you a signed declaration to that effect (Form 60 details). Sort this out in April, not during proof-submission week.

Also worth knowing: if your monthly rent exceeds **₹50,000**, you are required to deduct TDS at 2% under Section 194-IB before paying your landlord (older payments before October 2024 used 5%) — a rule that surprises many high-rent tenants in metros.

## Monthly, quarterly or yearly receipts?

There is no legal rule forcing monthly receipts — what matters is that receipts cover every month you claim. In practice:

- **Monthly receipts** are the safest default; every HR portal accepts them.
- **Quarterly receipts** (one receipt listing three months) are accepted by most employers and mean fewer signatures.
- **One annual receipt** is sometimes accepted, but a single receipt for ₹3,00,000 draws more scrutiny than twelve clean monthly ones.

## Common mistakes that get receipts rejected

- **Round-number mismatch**: receipts say ₹15,000 but your bank statement shows ₹14,500 going to the landlord every month.
- **Missing months**: claiming 12 months of HRA with 9 receipts.
- **No rent agreement**: many employers ask for the agreement alongside receipts; make one in minutes with the [rental agreement generator](/rental-agreement-generator) and see our guide to [writing a rent agreement](/blog/how-to-write-rent-agreement).
- **Paying parents rent without the paper trail**: paying rent to parents is legitimate, but it needs actual bank transfers, receipts, and the income appearing in the parent's ITR.
- **Same-day dates**: twelve receipts all signed and dated 10 January look exactly like what they are. Date each receipt in its own month — the generator does this automatically.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is an online rent receipt valid for HRA claims?

Yes. There is no requirement that a receipt be handwritten. A generated receipt with all required fields — names, address, amount, period, payment date — and the landlord's signature is fully valid for HRA proof.

### Do I need a revenue stamp on every rent receipt?

Only when the rent is paid in cash and exceeds ₹5,000. Payments by UPI, bank transfer or cheque need no revenue stamp, whatever the amount.

### When do I need my landlord's PAN?

When your total rent for the year exceeds ₹1,00,000. Below that, receipts alone are enough. If the landlord has no PAN, collect a signed declaration instead.

### Can I claim HRA if I pay rent to my parents?

Yes, if the arrangement is genuine: transfer the rent by bank or UPI, collect receipts, and your parent must declare the rent as income in their ITR. The house must be owned by the parent, not by you.

### Can I claim HRA without rent receipts?

For rent up to ₹3,000 per month, employers generally do not insist on receipts. Above that, receipts are required for the employer to allow the exemption — though if you miss the employer deadline, you can still claim HRA when filing your ITR, keeping receipts as evidence.

### Are rent receipts needed under the new tax regime?

No — the HRA exemption itself is not available under the new regime, so receipts serve no tax purpose there. They remain useful as address and payment proof.
