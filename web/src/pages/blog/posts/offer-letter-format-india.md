## What is an offer letter?

An offer letter is a formal written offer of employment from a company to a selected candidate. It confirms the role, compensation and joining date, and asks the candidate to accept within a deadline. Once accepted, it forms the basis of the employment contract, which is often followed by a more detailed appointment letter on joining.

## Offer letter vs appointment letter

| | Offer letter | Appointment letter |
|---|---|---|
| When issued | After selection, before joining | On or after the joining date |
| Purpose | Offers the job and key terms | Formally appoints and gives detailed terms |
| Length | 1-2 pages | Often 3-6 pages |
| Contents | Role, CTC, joining date, acceptance deadline | Duties, probation, leave, notice, code of conduct, confidentiality, termination |
| Signed by candidate | Yes, as acceptance | Yes, as agreement to terms |

## Essential elements of an offer letter

1. **Company letterhead:** logo, registered address, CIN/GSTIN if applicable.
2. **Date and reference number.**
3. **Candidate details:** full name and address.
4. **Job title and department.**
5. **Reporting manager and work location,** including the remote or hybrid policy.
6. **Date of joining.**
7. **Compensation:** annual CTC, with a salary breakup in an annexure (basic, HRA, special allowance, employer PF, etc.).
8. **Probation period:** commonly 3 to 6 months.
9. **Working hours and days.**
10. **Key benefits:** health insurance, leave, bonus or incentive eligibility.
11. **Conditions of the offer:** background verification, document submission, medical fitness if required.
12. **Notice period** during probation and after confirmation.
13. **Acceptance deadline and instructions.**
14. **Signature** of an authorised signatory, plus a space for the candidate's acceptance.

## Offer letter for freshers and interns

For freshers, keep the language simple and include:

- Training period details, if separate from probation
- Any service agreement or bond. Be reasonable and transparent, because courts look closely at bonds that are unreasonable.
- The documents needed: degree or provisional certificate, mark sheets, ID and address proof, PAN, bank details

For internships, issue a shorter letter with the stipend, duration and project details. At the end, give the intern a certificate using the [internship certificate generator](/internship-certificate-generator).

## Is an offer letter legally binding in India?

An accepted offer letter can create a binding contract under the Indian Contract Act, 1872, once both sides have agreed on the essential terms. In practice:

- Companies sometimes withdraw offers before joining. Candidates may have remedies, depending on the circumstances and the offer's terms.
- Candidates sometimes don't join after accepting. Employers rarely pursue this legally, but it can affect future hiring with that company.

Clear conditions and timelines in the letter reduce disputes on both sides. This is general information, not legal advice.

## Common mistakes in offer letters

1. **CTC with no breakup,** which leads to "my in-hand salary is lower than expected" disputes.
2. **Contradictory notice periods** between the offer and appointment letters.
3. **Missing conditions** such as background verification.
4. **Unsigned or undated letters.**
5. **Copy-paste errors:** the wrong candidate name, role or salary from a previous letter.
6. **Overly harsh restrictions,** for example post-employment non-competes, which are generally unenforceable in India under Section 27 of the Contract Act.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the format of an offer letter in India?

A standard offer letter is on company letterhead and includes the date and reference number, candidate details, job title, reporting manager, location, joining date, annual CTC with salary breakup, probation period, notice period, conditions of the offer, acceptance deadline and an authorised signature.

### What is the difference between an offer letter and an appointment letter?

An offer letter offers the job and key terms before joining. An appointment letter is issued on joining and formally appoints the employee with detailed terms of employment such as duties, leave, notice, confidentiality and termination.

### Is an offer letter legally valid without a signature?

An offer letter should be signed by an authorised signatory of the company and accepted in writing by the candidate. Unsigned letters are harder to rely on and may be rejected for background verification or loans.
