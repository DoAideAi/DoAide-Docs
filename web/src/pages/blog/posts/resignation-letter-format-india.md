A resignation letter is a short formal notice to your employer that you are leaving the job. In India it usually goes to your reporting manager with HR in CC, and it starts the clock on your **notice period**. A clean, polite letter protects your relieving letter, your experience letter and your professional reputation — all three of which your next employer may ask for.

This guide gives you ready-to-use **resignation letter formats for India**, including with notice period, for personal reasons and for immediate resignation. You can also create one on a clean letterhead in under a minute with the [free resignation letter generator](/resignation-letter-generator).

## What a resignation letter must include

Keep it short — three to five sentences is enough. Every resignation letter should contain:

| Element | Example |
|---|---|
| Date | 22 October 2026 |
| Addressee | Your reporting manager (CC: HR) |
| Clear statement of resignation | "I am resigning from my position as Software Engineer." |
| Last working day | As per your notice period in the appointment letter |
| Offer to help with handover | "I will ensure a smooth transition of my responsibilities." |
| A thank-you line | Keep it brief and gracious |
| Signature | Name, employee ID, contact details |

What to leave out: complaints about your manager, salary comparisons, the name of your new company, and emotional language. The letter goes into your employee file permanently — keep it neutral.

## Simple resignation letter format (with notice period)

> **Subject: Resignation – [Your Name], [Designation]**
>
> Dear [Manager's name],
>
> Please accept this letter as formal notice of my resignation from the position of [Designation] at [Company], effective today. As per my appointment letter, I will serve the [30/60/90]-day notice period, making my last working day [date].
>
> I will complete my pending work and ensure a smooth handover of my responsibilities during this period. Thank you for the opportunities and support during my time here.
>
> Regards,
> [Name] · [Employee ID] · [Phone]

## Resignation letter for personal reasons

When you don't want to state a specific reason, "personal reasons" is completely acceptable in India and no employer can insist on more detail:

> Dear [Manager's name],
>
> I am writing to resign from my position as [Designation] due to personal reasons, effective today. I will serve my full notice period and my last working day will be [date]. I am grateful for the guidance and opportunities I received at [Company], and I will do everything needed for a smooth handover.

## Immediate resignation letter (without serving notice)

Sometimes relocation, health or a family emergency makes serving notice impossible. Be upfront and ask for a waiver or buyout:

> Dear [Manager's name],
>
> Due to [reason — e.g., an urgent family situation], I must resign from my position as [Designation] with immediate effect. I understand my appointment letter requires a [X]-day notice period, and I request the company to waive it or adjust it against my pending leave balance / notice-period buyout as per policy. I apologise for the short notice and will assist remotely with handover wherever possible.

Note that the company can recover notice-period salary as per your appointment letter terms, and an unserved notice can delay your relieving letter — check your appointment letter before sending this one.

## Notice period rules in India: what you should know

- **Your notice period is contractual.** It is whatever your appointment letter says — typically 30 days for juniors, 60–90 days for senior roles. There is no single statutory notice period for private-sector employees in most cases.
- **Leave balance and buyout.** Many companies let you adjust earned leave against the notice period or buy it out (you or your new employer pays the shortfall). This is policy-dependent — ask HR in writing.
- **Resignation cannot be refused.** An employer can negotiate your last working day but cannot force you to stay indefinitely. Keep email proof of the date you resigned.
- **Documents to collect before you leave:** relieving letter, [experience letter](/experience-letter-generator), final salary slips, Form 16, and PF transfer/withdrawal details.

## After you resign: the paperwork checklist

1. Send the resignation letter by email so there is a timestamp, even if you also hand over a printed copy.
2. Get a written acknowledgement with your accepted last working day.
3. Complete the handover and get sign-off from your manager.
4. Collect your relieving letter and experience letter on or soon after the last day — generate reference drafts for HR with the [relieving letter generator](/relieving-letter-generator) and [experience letter generator](/experience-letter-generator).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best resignation letter format in India?

A short formal letter with the date, your designation, a clear statement of resignation, your last working day as per the notice period, an offer to help with handover, and a brief thank-you. Three to five sentences is ideal; avoid reasons, complaints or details about your next job.

### Can I resign by email in India?

Yes. An email to your reporting manager with HR in CC is a valid and common way to resign in India, and it creates a timestamped record. Some companies also ask you to resign in their HR portal — do both if required, and keep copies.

### Do I have to mention the reason for resignation?

No. "Personal reasons" or "career growth" is sufficient. You are not legally required to disclose the actual reason or the name of your next employer in a resignation letter.

### What happens if I don't serve my notice period?

The company can recover salary in lieu of the unserved notice as per your appointment letter, and may delay or withhold the relieving letter until dues are settled. Many companies allow a buyout or adjustment against earned leave — request it in writing.

### Can a company reject my resignation?

No. An employer can negotiate the last working day or ask you to serve the contractual notice, but cannot refuse a resignation outright or force you to continue working. Keep written proof of the date you submitted it.
